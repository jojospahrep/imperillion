How to play

  1. Each prompt has a hidden list of accepted answers about Imperial and South Ken. Every answer has an abundance: how many players we reckon would think of it.
  2. You get 25 seconds and one answer per prompt. If what you type isn't on the list, nothing launches and you can try again until the clock runs out.
  3. Your answer lifts off Queen's Lawn on a weather balloon. Rarer answers climb higher and score more.
  4. Daily climb is 7 prompts, the same for everyone, once a day. Unlimited keeps going. Hot Flask is a pass-the-phone party game.

Spelling is forgiving: small typos, abbreviations and surnames count. Abundance figures are hand-estimated, not live player data.
