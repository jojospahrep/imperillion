Imperillion

Find the rarest answer

Unofficial fan trivia. Not affiliated with Imperial College London.
Abundance figures are hand-estimated, not live player data.

How to play

  1. Each prompt has a list of accepted answers. Every answer has an abundance: the share of players we reckon would think of it.
  2. You get 5 guesses per prompt. A guess that isn't on the list still uses one up.
  3. Obvious answers score little. Obscure ones score big. Hit the single rarest answer and the round ends with a bonus.
  4. Spelling is forgiving: small typos, common abbreviations and surnames are accepted.
  5. Daily is three prompts, the same for everyone, once a day. Unlimited keeps dealing prompts.
  6. Party is Hot Flask, for 2–8 players sharing one device. Name an unused answer to pass the flask on. It boils over at a hidden moment 10–30 seconds after it's lit, and whoever holds it loses a life. Passing keeps the time that's left, but never less than 8 seconds. Saying the rarest answer wins a life back.
